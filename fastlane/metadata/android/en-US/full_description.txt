<p><i>Olauncher</i> is a minimal Android launcher with no ads or optional promotional messages.</p><p><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Minimal homescreen with configurable app shortcuts.</li><li>Text size, font, theme, alignment, status bar, and wallpaper settings.</li><li>Gestures for launching apps, searching, notifications, and locking the screen.</li><li>Widgets, hidden apps, dual-app support, and work-profile support.</li><li>No data collection. Open source under the GPLv3 license.</li></ul><p><b>Accessibility Service:</b> The optional service is used only for the double-tap-to-lock gesture and does not collect or share data.</p>
